Signing Instructions

Individual Applicant

Individual

Print the application, then sign it as follows:

Individual applicant: Sign the application and write the date.
Company: Affix the official company seal, and have a director of the company sign the application.
Partnership: Affix the partnership seal, and have the partners sign the application.
Proprietorship: Affix the proprietorship seal, and have the proprietor(s) sign the application.
Applying through a third party / Power of Attorney: The application must be signed by the person holding the power of attorney (the authorised representative).
